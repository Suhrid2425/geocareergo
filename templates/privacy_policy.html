Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

GeoCareerGo respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and services.

Information may be used to provide and improve our services, maintain user accounts, process job applications, provide career-related functionality, communicate with users and maintain platform security.

Companies may provide business information such as company name, website, professional social media links and verification documents. Such information may be used for company verification and recruitment-related functionality.

GeoCareerGo may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, provide functionality, improve the user experience and understand website usage.

Third-Party Services

GeoCareerGo may integrate third-party services such as authentication, analytics, hosting, advertising or external job application services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the platform. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the official contact channel available on GeoCareerGo.
